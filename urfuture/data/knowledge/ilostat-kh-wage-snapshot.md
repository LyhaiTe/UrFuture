ILOSTAT's Cambodia country profile tracks median monthly earnings by ISCO
occupational group. Within "Information and Communications Technology
Professionals" (ISCO-08 group 25), Cambodia's reported median monthly
earnings sit below regional ASEAN peers such as Vietnam and the Philippines,
reflecting a still-developing domestic tech sector concentrated mainly in
Phnom Penh and Siem Reap.

Entry-level software developer roles in Cambodia's formal ICT sector
typically fall in a wide band, with significant variance driven by whether
the employer is a local SME, an outsourcing/BPO firm serving foreign
clients, or a multinational's regional office — the latter two generally
paying above the domestic median.
