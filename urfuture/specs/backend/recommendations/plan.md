# Recommendations Plan

## Implemented flow
- Career recommendations are generated by `POST /api/career/recommend`.
- Job descriptions are evaluated by `POST /api/job/match`.
- Study plans are generated by `POST /api/study-plan/generate`.
- `guardrails.ts` identifies high-stakes/weakly grounded outputs.
- Counselor review records use `CounselorReview` and `ReviewStatus`.

## Stored evidence
Recommendations persist matched skills, missing skills, rationale, citations,
fit score, review requirement/status, and counselor notes.
